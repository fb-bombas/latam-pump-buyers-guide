# Country comparison matrix

Side-by-side summary of procurement factors across six LATAM countries.
Use as a starting screen — for any specific country, read that country's
chapter.

## Effective import-tax burden on industrial pumps

| Country | Effective burden on FOB | Notes |
|---------|------------------------|-------|
| Brazil | 40-60% | High II + IPI + state-variable ICMS |
| Argentina | 45-55% | Plus FX-approval risk |
| Mexico (USMCA origin) | 17-18% | If RoO met |
| Mexico (non-USMCA) | 27-32% | Standard tariff + IVA |
| Chile | 25-26% | Lowest in LATAM thanks to FTA network + low uniform tariff |
| Colombia | 25-35% | Varies by FTA origin |
| Peru | 22-30% | Uniform tariff + 18% IGV + 2% IPM |

## Local content / manufacturing strength

| Country | Local pump manufacturing | Bias toward domestic |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Brazil | Strong (~60-70% of installed pumps domestic) | Strong via BNDES Finame and state-procurement preference |
| Mexico | Moderate (Ruhrpumpen + others) | Moderate; USMCA shifts preference toward US/Canada |
| Argentina | Moderate (KSB-license + several domestic) | Strong via Mercosur preference + FX-control friction |
| Chile | Limited | Low; Chile is import-friendly |
| Colombia | Limited | Low to moderate |
| Peru | Limited (Hidrostal + others) | Low; mining majors import directly |

## Financing program parameters (approximate, 2026)

| Country | Program | Typical rate (nominal) | Term |
|---------|---------|------------------------|------|
| Brazil | BNDES Finame | TLP + 1.5-4.5% (≈ 9-12%) | Up to 10 years |
| Mexico | NAFIN Crédito Empresarial | TIIE + 3-7% (≈ 13-17%) | Up to 7 years |
| Argentina | BICE | USD-denominated (5-9% USD) | 5-7 years |
| Chile | CORFO | 6-10% | 5-10 years |
| Colombia | Bancóldex | 8-12% | 5-10 years |
| Peru | COFIDE | 9-13% | 5-10 years |

Argentine peso financing is structurally too volatile for capital-
equipment terms, so most procurement uses USD-denominated structures.

## Typical procurement timeline (RFQ to commissioning)

| Country | Local sourcing | Imported sourcing |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| Brazil | 21-34 weeks | 29-46 weeks |
| Mexico (USMCA import) | 22-32 weeks | 26-38 weeks |
| Argentina | 22-30 weeks | 32-50 weeks (incl. FX delay) |
| Chile | 24-32 weeks | 28-44 weeks (longer for engineered slurry) |
| Colombia | 24-32 weeks | 28-40 weeks |
| Peru | 26-35 weeks | 32-50 weeks (altitude logistics) |

## Dominant industrial demand drivers

| Country | Largest pump-demand sectors |
|---------|------------------------------|
| Brazil | Oil & gas (Petrobras + private), water/sanitation, food, sugar/ethanol, mining |
| Mexico | Oil & gas (PEMEX + private), automotive, food, water utilities |
| Argentina | Vaca Muerta oil & gas, food/agriculture, mining (rapid growth), petrochemicals |
| Chile | Mining (~50-60%), water utilities, food |
| Colombia | Oil & gas, water utilities, food/coffee/sugar, agro-export |
| Peru | Mining (~50%+), oil & gas, agro-irrigation, fishing |

## Mercosur trade access

For Brazilian and Argentine exporters:

| Origin | Destination | Mercosur preference |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| Brazil | Argentina | Yes (full Mercosur) |
| Brazil | Mexico | Partial (Mercosur-Mexico ACE) |
| Brazil | Chile | Partial (Mercosur-Chile ACE) |
| Brazil | Colombia | Partial (Mercosur-Andean ACE) |
| Brazil | Peru | Partial (Mercosur-Andean ACE) |
| Argentina | Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay | Yes |
| Argentina | Chile/Colombia/Peru | Partial |

ACE = Acuerdo de Complementación Económica. Tariff reduction varies by
NCM code. For pumps, typical reduction is 50-100% of standard tariff.

## Strategic recommendations summary

| If procurement priority is... | Best country dynamic |
|---------------------------------|----------------------|
| Lowest landed cost on imports | Chile (FTA + low base tariff) |
| Domestic-vendor depth | Brazil |
| FTA optimization | Chile, Mexico, Colombia, Peru |
| Mercosur-protected market | Brazil, Argentina (intra-Mercosur) |
| Mining-engineered specialty | Chile or Peru |
| Lowest-FX-risk procurement | Chile (CLP + USD stability) |
| Oil-and-gas-dominant | Mexico (post-reform), Brazil |
